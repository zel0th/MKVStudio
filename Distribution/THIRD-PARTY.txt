MKVStudio — bundled console tools

MKVStudio runs mkvmerge, mkvinfo, mkvextract and mkvpropedit as separate
processes. It does not link its own executable against these programs or Qt.
MKVStudio's original source code remains under the MIT license.

Console tools: version 102.0, GPL-2.0-or-later, copyright Moritz Bunkus and
contributors. The official macOS binaries are provided by Graham Thompson.
Qt Core: version 6.11.1, copyright The Qt Company Ltd. and contributors;
LGPL-3.0 / GPL licensing options and third-party notices are retained in the
included Qt source archive. See Licenses for extracted license texts.

Only the four console programs and their recursive non-system dynamic
library dependencies are copied from the pinned upstream DMG. The GUI,
GUI plugins and unused Qt libraries are excluded. Binaries are not patched
or relinked; their local ad-hoc signatures are renewed for this app bundle.

Sources/ contains the matching upstream console-tool and Qt Base source
archives, including build instructions, macOS packaging scripts and
third-party code/notices. sources.json records URLs, versions and SHA-256
checksums; payload.json records hashes after local signing. License texts
are also provided separately under Licenses/ and COPYING-GPL-2.0.txt.

The console tools use a replaceable dynamic Qt Core library under
Contents/Helpers/ConsoleTools/libs. Modified tools/libraries can be rebuilt
from their upstream sources and used via MKVStudio Settings, or substituted
in a local copy of the bundle and re-signed. There is no DRM or online
activation check. The full upstream distributions and their licenses remain
available from https://mkvtoolnix.download/ and https://download.qt.io/.
